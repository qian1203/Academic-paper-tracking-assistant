### [1] [Purely analytic composites: Relative variance contributions of indicators corresponding to a priori indicator weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27597)
*Andre Beauducel,Ned Kock*

Main category: stat.AP

TL;DR: 本文提出"纯分析复合指标"概念，通过调整指标权重使其方差贡献精确匹配预设值，解决了传统分析复合指标中方差贡献受指标方差和相关性影响的问题，并给出R脚本实现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统分析复合指标中，指标的方差贡献会受到指标本身方差和指标间相关性的影响，导致实际方差贡献与预设权重不一致，需要一种能使方差贡献精确匹配预设权重的复合指标构建方法。

Method: 提出"纯分析复合指标"(purely analytic composites)，通过数学方法调整使得指标在复合指标中的方差贡献精确等于预设的权重值，并基于模拟数据展示传统分析复合指标与纯分析复合指标的差异。

Result: 通过模拟数据示例证明了纯分析复合指标与传统分析复合指标的差异，前者能确保指标的方差贡献精确匹配预设权重。

Conclusion: 纯分析复合指标可用于需要精确控制指标贡献的场景，如交易所交易基金(ETF)领域，文中提供了R脚本以便计算。

Abstract: Composites are often created to facilitate the work of decision-makers. Therefore, practical or theoretical considerations may lead to a priori weights of the indicators forming a composite. Composites that are created a weighted aggregates are not the result of data analysis and may therefore be termed 'analytic composites'. However, it has already been shown that the variance contributions of indicators within analytic composites are affected by the indicator variance and indicator inter-correlations. In the present study purely analytic composites are proposed, having exactly the variance contribution of indicators within the composites that are a priori defined by the indicator weights. An example based on simulated data illustrates the difference between analytic composites and purely analytic composites. As an application area, we propose that purely analytic composites could be of interest in the exchange-traded fund. An R-script for the computation of purely analytic composites is given in the Appendix.

### [2] [Why pyrotechnics markets keep killing:a simple geometric argument for redesign](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27648)
*Carlos M. Hernandez-Suarez,Alonso Sanchez-Maldonado,Carlos A. Robles-Hernandez*

Main category: stat.AP

TL;DR: 论文指出，烟花零售市场的全球拓扑结构（整体布局）是导致火灾伤亡的主导因素，通过火势蔓延和人员疏散两个几何渠道影响死亡率。现有标准只关注局部参数（如过道宽度），忽略全局拓扑，作者提出应从空间生态学的种子传播模型中借鉴，设计能同时减缓火势蔓延和缩短疏散距离的市场几何结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 全球烟花零售市场火灾频发，单次事故可导致数十至数百人死亡，而现有安全标准仅关注局部参数，忽视市场整体布局对伤亡的决定性影响，亟需从拓扑层面提出系统性解决方案。

Method: 通过分析火势传播（引燃物品的弹道散布概率与摊位空间密度的关系）和人员疏散（疏散距离由市场整体足迹而非单个摊位决定）两个几何渠道，运用空间生态学中的接触过程模型（种子传播模型）来推导最优市场几何结构。

Result: 死亡率风险随疏散时间呈近似指数增长，拓扑结构将疏散距离的微小差异放大为伤亡数量的巨大差异。当前美国、欧盟、墨西哥的法规仅规定过道宽度、摊位间距等局部参数，未对全局拓扑进行监管。

Conclusion: 市场拓扑应成为可监管的设计变量，并提出一种能同时减缓火势蔓延和缩短疏散距离的市场几何布局方案。

Abstract: Fires and explosions in pyrotechnics retail markets recur worldwide with predictable regularity, killing dozens to hundreds of people in single events. This paper argues that the global topology of the market is the dominant determinant of mortality, acting through two independent geometric channels. The first, propagation, concerns ballistic dispersal of ignited articles: the probability that fire spreads between blocks scales with the spatial density of blocks within the dispersal range. The second, evacuation, concerns the distance an occupant must traverse to reach the perimeter, which is set by the global geometry of the market footprint, not by any stall-level parameter. Because mortality risk grows approximately exponentially in evacuation time, topology amplifies modest differences in egress distance into large differences in casualties. Current standards in the United States, the European Union, and Mexico prescribe local parameters such as aisle width and stall separation, but leave the global topology of the market unregulated. We argue that topology should be a regulable design variable, and propose a market geometry that simultaneously slows propagation and shortens evacuation, derived from contact-process models of seed dispersal in spatial ecology.

### [3] [Likelihood-Free Inference for Multivariate Generalized Pareto Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27694)
*Samira Aka,Marie Kratz,Philippe Naveau*

Main category: stat.AP

TL;DR: 提出了一种两阶段无似然推断方法AW-NBE，结合神经贝叶斯估计与Sinkhorn最优传输细化，用于多元极值模型参数推断。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多元极值模型的似然推断常因似然函数难处理或支持离散而不可靠，尤其对于多元离散广义帕累托模型，需从稀疏超阈值样本中同时推断边缘尾部行为和依赖结构。

Method: 两阶段无似然推断：第一阶段用模拟数据训练神经贝叶斯估计器获得初始参数估计；第二阶段通过最小化观测与模拟超阈值数据经验分布之间的Sinkhorn散度进行局部细化。

Result: 在金融对数收益率和瑞士干旱期超阈值数据上，AW-NBE相比单独使用Sinkhorn散度、标准神经贝叶斯估计器或删失似然估计，均能改进参数推断。

Abstract: Likelihood-based inference for multivariate extreme-value models is often unreliable or infeasible when likelihoods are intractable or supports are discrete. This challenge is particularly acute for multivariate discrete generalized Pareto models, where both marginal tail behavior and dependence must be inferred from sparse exceedance samples. We propose a two-stage likelihood-free inference procedure, termed AW--NBE (Adaptive Wasserstein Neural Bayes Estimator), that combines neural Bayes estimation with a targeted optimal transport refinement step based on the Sinkhorn discrepancy. In the first stage, a neural Bayes estimator trained on simulated data provides fast and stable initial parameter estimates. In the second stage, these estimates are locally refined by minimizing the Sinkhorn divergence between the empirical distributions of observed and simulated exceedances. This refinement reduces the Sinkhorn discrepancy between the empirical distributions of observed and simulated exceedances, while preserving dependence features learned by the neural estimator. Model adequacy is assessed using new optimal transport based multivariate Q--Q and potential diagnostics. Applications to financial log-returns and Swiss dry spell exceedances suggest that AW--NBE can improve parameter inferences compared to estimation using solely, either the Sinkhorn discrepancy, or the standard neural Bayes estimators and censored likelihood estimation.
